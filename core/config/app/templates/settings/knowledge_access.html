Knowledge access

Who can read each knowledge scope, and why

A knowledge scope is an Authentik group named "KB: <scope>" with the attribute rzfz_kb_scope: true. Its members read it; so does the owner of each dataset in it. A Dify dataset joins a scope through a knowledge tag with the group's name; a cognee dataset through the group's attribute rzfz_kb_targets.cognee_datasets. In Open WebUI the knowledge owner shares a knowledge base with the scope's group. The role reconcile applies this on every refresh; a dataset taken out of every scope goes back to owner-only.

Dify limits: the dataset permission is enforced in the Dify console only. The workspace owner sees every dataset, and a Dify app that uses a dataset retrieves from it for everyone who may run the app, whatever the scope. Share an app built on a scoped dataset only with that scope's members.
